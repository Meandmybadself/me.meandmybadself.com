In August of 2016, I was fascinated by the Twitter Streaming API.  I was very enamored with the work of Stamen, particularly their [MTV Movie Awards](https://stamen.com/kanye-west-is-an-a-hole-and-other-twitter-moments-from-the-mtv-video-music-awards-603e16317bab/) project, visualizing Kanye's snatch of Taylor's mic.

I'd built a handful of silly experiments watching real-time Twitter traffic. 

## Minnecrapoli.st
A website that rendered geo-located tweets from the Twin Cities area, visualizing:
* Sentiment (color of circle)
* Reach (size of circle)
* The most frequently tweeting users
* Most frequently used emojis

Ultimately, I had to retire it when Google Maps moved from version 2 to 3, breaking older integrations.

### Story
We had this running in the Permanent Record office on April 21, 2016, the day that Prince died & saw a massive sea of red dots paint the cities.
